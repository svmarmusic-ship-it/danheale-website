The Problem

Suno tracks can't be commercially released

Labels and distributors are getting serious about AI-generated content. DistroKid, TuneCore, and most major label A&R departments now require submissions to be 100% human-produced — and they're getting better at detecting when they're not.

A track built on Suno — even with a strong vocal or a strong hook over it — hits a wall. The instrumental is AI-generated, and that disqualifies it from most commercial release pathways before it's even heard.

  • ◆
    Distribution rejections — major distributors flag AI-generated instrumentals and can pull releases post-approval, after you've already promoted them.
  • ◆
    Label submission barriers — A&R teams at most imprints won't consider demos with AI elements in the production chain, regardless of how strong the concept is.
  • ◆
    Sync licensing blocked — TV, film, and ad placements require full human authorship and a clean IP chain. AI-generated stems don't qualify.
  • ◆
    Platform risk — Spotify and Apple Music are tightening AI content policies. Tracks flagged post-release face removal or reduced visibility.
